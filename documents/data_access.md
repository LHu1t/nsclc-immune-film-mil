## Data Source

Whole-slide images and the corresponding metadata were obtained from The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).

Access:
- TCGA LUAD
- TCGA LUSC

Data portal:
https://portal.gdc.cancer.gov/

Feature embeddings were generated by the Mahmood Lab pathology foundation model pipeline.

Due to data redistribution restrictions, feature files are not included. Users must download or regenerate features independently.

Data access must be granted and can be downloaded from the Mahmood Lab: https://huggingface.co/datasets/MahmoodLab/UNI2-h-features/tree/main/TCGA.
